## Mining Shift Management and Safety System

A web and mobile app designed to improve productivity and safety in coal mining operations by digitalizing shift handovers and managing Safety Management Plans (SMP) as per DGMS guidelines.

# Features:
- **Digital Shift Logs:** Streamlined shift handover with detailed logs and activity tracking.
- **Safety Management:** Hazard identification, risk assessment, and control measure tracking.
- **Real-Time Notifications:** Alerts for safety issues and critical tasks.
- **Report Generation:** Export shift logs and SMP data as PDF reports.
